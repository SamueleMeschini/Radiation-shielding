First-interaction probabilities from the configured photon library.

pj = μj/μtotal. These are not end-of-history fractions. Blue: coherent; orange: Compton; green: photoelectric; purple: pair/triplet. Dashed vertical lines mark K-shell binding energies from the photon library; connecting lines guide the eye between discrete evaluated energies.

Physics behind this result

E′ = E/[1+(E/0.51099895 MeV)(1−cos θ)]; Te = E−E′. Te,max = 2E²/(0.51099895+2E); E′π = E/[1+2E/0.51099895]. At exactly 0°, the photon has no energy or direction change and is experimentally indistinguishable from an uncollided photon.

Pair-production threshold: 1.0219979 MeV. Above threshold, Te−+Te+ ≈ Eγ−1.0219979 MeV, neglecting nuclear recoil.

Physics behind this result

TN = ΣwᵢTN,i; TE = ΣwᵢEᵢTE,i/ΣwᵢEᵢ. Mean outgoing energy = ΣwᵢEᵢTE,i/ΣwᵢTN,i. For an interval, μeff = −ln[I(x₂)/I(x₁)]/(x₂−x₁); I(x₂)/I(x₁) is incremental transmission, while I(x₂)/I(0) is cumulative.

Physics behind this result

For uncollided photons, T = exp[−Σμᵢxᵢ] = exp[−Σ(μ/ρ)ᵢmA,i]. Order does not affect this expression, but it can affect scattered and secondary-photon spectra. Spectrum bins are energy intervals, not unique interaction-mechanism labels.

Beta graded-Z ordering

Source → low-Z → high-Z usually puts the lower bremsstrahlung producer first, then attenuates photons farther out.

Source → high-Z → low-Z produces more bremsstrahlung close to the source. This qualitative comparison is not a transport result.

Physics behind this result

Mass CSDA range Rm = ∫₀ᴱ dE/[S(E)/ρ]; physical range R = Rm/ρ. Mass range removes trivial density scaling, but varies with composition. Where ESTAR collision and radiative stopping powers are available, frad = (1/E₀)∫₀ᴱ Srad/(Scol+Srad)dE. The rough screening formula fB ≈ 3.5×10−4ZeffEmax is approximate, not transport. Zeff = ΣnᵢZᵢ²/ΣnᵢZᵢ and is process-dependent.

Local NIST ESTAR/ASTAR tables are bundled for the materials shown. Numerical integration starts at the first tabulated energy using the NIST CSDA range there as its low-energy anchor. Missing material and energy combinations are withheld.
